Terms of Service

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. What the app is

ViceClips Studio is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of the ViceClips FR TikTok account. It edits short vertical gaming clips on the owner's own computer and, with the owner's explicit authorization, sends finished videos to the owner's TikTok drafts (inbox). The owner then reviews, edits and publishes each video himself inside the TikTok app.

2. Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, with his own TikTok account.

3. TikTok connection

  • The app connects to TikTok only after the user logs in and authorizes it through TikTok's official login screen.
  • The app only uploads videos to the user's drafts. It never publishes on its own and never posts without the user finishing the post in TikTok.
  • The user can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

4. User responsibilities

The user is responsible for the content he publishes, including respecting TikTok's Community Guidelines, copyright and the rights of the creators whose content is used (credit, permission or participation in authorized clipping programs).

5. No warranty

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Data we access

When the user authorizes the app, TikTok provides only the data allowed by the requested scopes:

  • user.info.basic: the user's display name and avatar, to show which account is connected;
  • video.upload: permission to send videos to the user's TikTok drafts.

2. How data is used

This data is used only to show the connected account and to upload videos chosen by the user to his own drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage

Access tokens and videos are stored only on the user's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not send data to any third party other than TikTok's official API.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

5. Deletion

The user can delete all data by revoking the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) and by deleting the app's folder on his computer.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children and is used only by its owner.

Questions about this policy: contact the owner through the TikTok account @viceclips_fr0.
